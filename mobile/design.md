# AiMarket NEXUS Mobile Design

AiMarket NEXUS is a portrait-first marketing operating system for one-handed use. Its native visual language uses NEXUS Navy (`#0B1220`), Signal Blue (`#2563EB`), Sky Accent (`#EAF2FF`), Canvas (`#F7F9FC`), Ink (`#14213D`), Growth Green (`#16A34A`), Signal Amber (`#D97706`), and Risk Red (`#DC2626`).

| Screen | Primary content and function |
|---|---|
| Login | Secure email/password session through the AiMarket production API. |
| Home | Live spend, revenue, lead KPIs, campaign watch, and quick actions. |
| Campaigns | Campaign list, create flow, detail view, status and metric updates. |
| Plan | Strategy, Content Studio, and SEO intelligence segments. |
| Intelligence | Leads, Revenue, Competitor Radar, and Business Brain segments. |
| Automate | AI Agents, Missions, Learning, and Governance segments. |
| Workspace | Profile, location-currency settings, autopilot preference, and sign-out. |

The primary flow is sign in, review the dashboard, enter a tab workspace, create or review marketing work, and return to the dashboard. Workspace settings are opened from the Home avatar. Segments stay horizontally reachable with a thumb, and primary actions appear after their supporting context.
