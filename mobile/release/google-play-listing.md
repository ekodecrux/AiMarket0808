## Store copy

### Short description

Run your marketing command center: campaigns, AI insights, missions, and approvals in one place.

### Full description

AiMarket NEXUS is the mobile command center for teams that want to operate marketing with focus and control. Review campaign performance, manage live metrics, create strategic plans, generate content, and coordinate SEO work from one clear workspace.

Use Intelligence to prioritize leads, review revenue signals, monitor competitors, and ask grounded questions through Business Brain. Use Automate to supervise scheduled AI agents, create marketing missions, generate learning reports, and set governance policies that match your team’s approval process.

AiMarket NEXUS keeps your workspace context aligned with your operating location and currency. When a connection is interrupted, it continues to show your most recently saved workspace data and offers a clear retry action when you are back online.

Enable approval alerts to be notified when a mission changes status or an agent run needs your attention. You stay in control of marketing automation while retaining the context needed to make quick, informed decisions.
